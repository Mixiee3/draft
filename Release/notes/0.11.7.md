# Draft 0.11.7 — Liquid Glass editor polish

- The formatting toolbar is real Liquid Glass. It stays inside the editor and keeps its icons readable over light and dark pages.
- A new block bar at the bottom of the page selects the current block and has quick actions to turn it into another block type, style it, copy a link to it, or open the full block menu. It steps aside while you type.
- In the document sidebar, press on a tab and slide across the others to switch; the selected tab keeps its icon.
- Clicking empty space below your text, or around the page, now stops typing.
- Accent is a clean underline, and Glow is a soft light with a clear centre, on headings, paragraphs and lists alike.
- Tags no longer get a box behind them, and removing a block colour no longer leaves an empty box.
- No note content or vault format changes.
