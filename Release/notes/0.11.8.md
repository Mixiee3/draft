# Draft 0.11.8 — Liquid Glass controls

- The document sidebar's tab selector is now Liquid Glass. Press and slide across the tabs and a glass lens flows over the icons, then settles on the one you chose.
- New switches throughout Draft: a bright accent track, and a knob that stretches as you press it and springs across.
- The buttons above a note (Back, Insert, Share, Format, Style, Info) and the folder chip are Liquid Glass and take on the colours of the note's background.
- The selected sidebar tab is easier to see in light mode.
- No note content or vault format changes.
